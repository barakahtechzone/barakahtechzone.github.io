Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Answers Grounded in Your Data

RAG connects your knowledge base to an LLM so responses are accurate, cited and free from hallucination. We build end-to-end RAG pipelines — from document ingestion to production-grade retrieval.

RAG Pipeline Architecture

Ingest & Chunk

Documents, databases and APIs are chunked into semantically meaningful segments and stored with metadata.

Embed & Index

Each chunk is converted into a high-dimensional vector and indexed in a vector database for fast similarity search.

Retrieve & Rank

User queries are embedded, matched against the index and ranked by relevance to find the most useful context.

Generate & Ground

The LLM receives retrieved context alongside the query, producing answers grounded in your actual data.

Model Fine-Tuning

A Model That Speaks Your Domain

Fine-tune foundation models on your specific data, terminology and workflows. Get domain expert performance without the cost of training from scratch — using LoRA, RLHF and instruction tuning.

Fine-Tuning Pipeline Architecture

Data Preparation

Curate high-quality instruction/response pairs from your domain. Clean, format and validate training data for maximum signal.

LoRA / QLoRA Tuning

Efficient parameter-efficient fine-tuning using LoRA adapters — full customization without retraining the entire model.

Evaluation & QA

Benchmark the tuned model against held-out test sets, measure accuracy, relevance and safety before deployment.

Deploy & Iterate

Push to production with A/B testing infrastructure. Monitor drift and retrain as your domain evolves.
