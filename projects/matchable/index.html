Matchable

An AI-powered matching engine that connects people, opportunities or resources using reasoning over structured data.

Overview

Matchable is an AI-driven matching engine that reasons over structured data to connect the right people, opportunities or resources together.

The Challenge

Effective matching requires more than keyword filters — it needs contextual reasoning over preferences, constraints and signals that change over time.

The Solution

Matchable combines a retrieval layer with AI reasoning to score and rank matches, with a feedback loop that improves relevance as more interactions are observed.

Key Features

  • AI reasoning over matching criteria
  • Retrieval-augmented candidate scoring
  • Configurable matching domains
  • Feedback-driven relevance improvement
  • Explainable match results

Engineering Highlights

  • Retrieval-augmented matching pipeline
  • AI reasoning over structured constraints
  • Feedback loop for relevance tuning
